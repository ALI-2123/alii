Mods

Mods for Minecraft . Every version has its own mods folder.

Fabric loads mods in Minecraft 1.14 and newer.

Add mods

The Blade mod (Minecraft 1.21.1, 1.21.4 and 1.21.11) adds the Right Shift PvP HUD, red and black menus, wings, blade, friends and chat in game. It needs Fabric and Fabric API, which the performance pack installs.

The pack adds Fabric API, Sodium, Sodium Extra, Lithium, FerriteCore, Entity Culling, More Culling, ImmediatelyFast, ModernFix, Krypton, C2ME, Noisium, ScalableLux, Dynamic FPS, MemoryLeakFix, and BadOptimizations from Modrinth. Any mod without a build for your version is skipped.
